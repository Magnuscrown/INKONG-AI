Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

We do not sell your data. We do not save your data on our servers. INKONG AI runs in your browser, and there are no accounts.

What stays on your device

Your chats, saved searches and saved videos are stored only in your browser. The AI runs on your device, so what you type to it is not sent to us. When you turn audio or video into text, the file is processed on your device and is not uploaded. Clearing your browser data deletes all of it, and we cannot recover it.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. We do not collect the content of your chats or the files you attach.

Third parties you may connect to

Some features work through other services, and they have their own privacy policies:

Search: the words you search for are sent to Wikipedia to get results.

Videos: videos are played by YouTube, which may collect information according to its own policy.

AI download: the first time you use the AI, its files are downloaded from public content networks, which see basic connection details such as your IP address.

Your own Gemini key (optional): if you add one, your messages are sent from your browser to Google, and Google's terms apply. Your key is stored only on your device and we never see it.

Hosting: the service that hosts this site may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of visit, for security.

Ads and cookies

We may show ads from partner advertising networks. These partners may use cookies to show and measure ads. We do not give them your chats or anything you type. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can delete your chats and saves at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can skip the optional Gemini key and use only the on-device AI.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
