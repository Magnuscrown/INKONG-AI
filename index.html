Privacy Policy

INKONG AI is built on a single principle: your data belongs to you. The entire application runs inside your web browser, which means we never receive, store, process, or transmit any information you enter — not your messages, not your prompts, not your uploads, and not your settings.

Your chat history, saved conversations, and preferences live exclusively in your device's local storage, and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser data or deleting the tab's storage.

We collect no account information because there is no account to create. We use no tracking pixels, no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party advertising identifiers. Nothing you type here is ever sold, shared, rented, or exposed to any external party.

If a feature ever requires an external API call (such as image generation), only the exact input you supply is sent — never your identity, device fingerprint, or browsing history.

By using INKONG AI, you accept that your data lives on your device alone. If you lose your device, clear your browser, or switch browsers, your data will not follow you — because we never had a copy to begin with. That is the trade: absolute privacy in exchange for zero cloud backup. We believe that's a fair deal, and we wouldn't have it any other way.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need an account to use INKONG AI?

No. INKONG AI has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. Everything runs directly in your browser the moment you open the page.

2. Is INKONG AI really free?

Yes — every tool is 100% free, with no paywalls, no trial limits, and no hidden premium tiers. Fact-checking, image generation, deepfake detection, long video summarization, and smart learning are all open to everyone.

3. Where is my data stored?

Only inside your own browser's local storage. Your chats, prompts, and settings never touch our servers because there are no servers involved. Clearing your browser data or the tab's storage permanently deletes your history.

4. Do you have a mobile app?

Not yet — but a native mobile application is in active development and will be released in the future. For now, INKONG AI works beautifully on any modern mobile browser as a Progressive Web App you can install straight to your home screen.

5. Can INKONG AI see what I type or paste?

No. Your words, links, and uploads stay inside your device unless you explicitly choose to send them to a model API. We collect no analytics, no fingerprints, and no personal identifiers.
