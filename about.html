About INKONG AI

INKONG AI is an all-in-one hub. Private AI chat, web search, audio and video to text, free videos and games, all in one place.

Built on privacy

The AI runs inside your own browser, on your own phone or laptop. What you type stays on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not sell your data.

Why we built it

Most AI tools ask you to sign up, send everything you type to a server and pay or be tracked. INKONG AI is built differently: open it, enter, and start. Fast, free and private by design.

Who is behind it

INKONG AI was founded by Magnus Crown, Founder & CEO. Designed for people everywhere.

What is next

We are improving the AI, adding more tools and games, and planning a mobile app.
