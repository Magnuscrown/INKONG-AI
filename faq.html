Frequently Asked Questions

What is INKONG AI?

An all-in-one hub with private AI chat, web search, audio and video to text, free videos and games in one place.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, tap Enter and start. There is no sign-up.

Is it free?

Yes. INKONG AI is free to use.

How do you manage my data?

We do not sell it and we do not save it on our servers. Your chats, searches and saves stay in your own browser.

Can you see what I type?

No. The AI runs in your browser on your device, so we never see your chats. Two exceptions: search words are sent to Wikipedia to get results, and if you choose to add your own Gemini key, your messages go to Google.

Why does the AI download when I first enter?

The AI runs on your device instead of our servers. It downloads once, is saved in your browser, and opens much faster next time. Wi-Fi is best.

How do I delete my chats?

Open the menu to see your saved chats. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Is the AI always right?

No. AI can make mistakes, so double-check anything important.

Which devices does it work on?

Most modern phones and laptops. Weaker devices automatically use a lighter AI so the app stays smooth, and very old devices can use their own free Gemini key.

Do you have a mobile app?

Not yet. We are planning one for the future. For now, use INKONG AI in your browser.
